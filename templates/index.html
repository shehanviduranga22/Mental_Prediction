About MindMirror

Mind Mirror is an artificial intelligence powered platform designed to help users reflect on their emotional experiences through natural language.

The system analyses the information provided by users and identifies meaningful emotional patterns and contextual signals. Instead of replacing professional mental health services, Mind Mirror aims to provide an accessible first step toward self-awareness and reflection.

The conceptual vision for this project was developed by Pramudi Nawanjana (BSc. Honours Degree in Computer Science and Technology).

The technical development of the platform was led by Shehan Viduranga (BSc. Honours Degree in Computer Science and Technology) and Kaveesha Ridmi (BSc. Honours Degree in Computer Science and Technology).

Through this collaboration, the team combines artificial intelligence, natural language processing and thoughtful user experience design to create a platform that encourages users to better understand their thoughts and emotions.
